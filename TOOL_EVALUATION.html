The User Journey

1

What is a "café" in planning terms?

Helpful

Tool: get_definition

Query: {"term": "cafe"}

Result: Not found, but suggested "restaurant or cafe" as the correct term.

Verdict: Guided user to the right answer instead of just failing.

2

Get the proper definition

Excellent

Tool: get_definition

Result: Clear definition explaining it's a "food and drink premises" with seating for on-premises consumption.

3

What zones are available?

Good

Tool: list_zones

Result: 17 active zones categorized by type, with note about legacy codes (B1→E1, etc.)

4

Can I open a café in E2 (CBD)?

Issue Found

Tool: check_permissibility

Problem: Said "likely permitted" via catch-all, instead of recognizing café is permitted under "Commercial premises".

Root Cause: Didn't understand hierarchical land uses: cafe → restaurant or cafe → food and drink premises → commercial premises

Critical Bug Fixed

Implemented hierarchical term matching so the tool now correctly recognizes that "café" falls under "Commercial premises".

Before Fix
{
  "permissibility": "likely_permitted_with_consent",
  "detail": "not explicitly listed but Zone E2 permits
             'any other development not specified'"
}
After Fix
{
  "permissibility": "permitted_with_consent",
  "detail": "'cafe' falls under 'Commercial premises'
             which is permitted in Zone E2",
  "classification": "'cafe' is a type of 'commercial premises'"
}
5

Parking requirements

Excellent

Tool: get_parking_rates

Result: Direct answer: 1 space per 10m² dining area.

6

DA fees for $50k fitout

Excellent

Tool: calculate_da_fees

Result: $355.00 statutory fee. Notes additional fees may apply (advertising, s7.11 contributions).

7

What documents do I need?

Excellent

Result: 6 base documents + 3 type-specific + 6 conditional documents with clear triggers.

8

How do I write the SEE?

Good

Tool: get_see_template

9

Flood requirements for CBD

Good

Tool: get_flood_requirements

Result: FPL: 1% AEP + 500mm freeboard. CBD exemption conditions listed. Advises consulting Duty Planner.

10

Check referrals (heritage building)

Good

Tool: check_referrals

Result: Heritage Council and Transport NSW referrals triggered. Shows required documents for each.

11

Who do I contact?

Excellent

Tool: get_contact_info

Result: Phone, address, hours. Free Duty Planner: Tuesdays/Thursdays 8:30-10:30am, no appointment needed.

Workflow Assessment

User Question Tool Support Gap
"What can I build here?" Partial Can't lookup property constraints from address
"Is my use permitted?" Good FIXED - hierarchical matching now works

"What parking do I need?" Good No CBD reduction variations
"What will it cost?" Good Fees accurate, no fit-out cost guidance
"Any special approvals?" Good Referral triggers clear
"Who do I contact?" Excellent Duty planner details included

Remaining Gaps

  • No property lookup — Can't determine zone, flood status, or heritage listing from an address. Requires NSW Planning Portal API or Council data.
  • No flood map data — Can't check if specific site is flood-affected. Returns general requirements only.
  • No heritage schedule search — Schedule 5 is in LEP text but not indexed by property address.
  • No CBD parking variations — Doesn't mention contribution-in-lieu options or shared parking arrangements.
