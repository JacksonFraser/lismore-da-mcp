LISMORE LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL PLAN 2012 - REG 4.1 Minimum subdivision lot size
LISMORE LOCAL ENVIRONMENTAL PLAN 2012 - REG 4.1
Minimum subdivision lot size
4.1 MINIMUM SUBDIVISION LOT SIZE

(1) The objectives of this clause are as follows-
(a) to provide a minimum lot size for the subdivision of land,
(b) to ensure that lot sizes have a practical and efficient layout to meet the intended use of each lot.
(2) This clause applies to a subdivision of any land shown on the Lot Size Map
(3) The size of any lot resulting from a subdivision of land to which this clause applies is not to be less than the minimum size shown on the Lot Size Map
(4) This clause does not apply in relation to the subdivision of any land-
(a) by the registration of a strata plan or strata plan of subdivision under the Strata Schemes Development Act 2015
(b) by any kind of subdivision under the Community Land Development Act 2021
